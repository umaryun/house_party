# House Party · launch kit

**Video:** `video/out/house-party-launch.mp4`. It's 40s, 1080×1920 (9:16) and 30fps, with music and sound effects. All key text is on screen, so it works with the sound off.

## Why it's built this way

| Choice | Reason |
|---|---|
| Opens on a **"POV:"** line that's on screen from frame 0 | POV hooks get outsized results on TikTok/Reels, and a big share of first views are muted |
| The product shows up by **3s** (the house reveal) | Showing the payoff early keeps viewers past the drop-off window |
| **4 phones, 4 countries, 1 party** | Shows "different places, same party" in one image |
| Cuts land on the beat every 1.5–2s | Fast cuts hold attention better than long shots |
| One CTA: **Play free · link in bio** | One low-commitment next step converts best |
| Text kept inside the safe area (no key copy in the bottom ~300px or the right edge) | TikTok's caption and buttons cover those zones |

## Posting plan
1. Post the same video natively to TikTok, Reels and Shorts. Don't cross-post with watermarks.
2. Test 2–3 of the hooks above as separate posts over the first week, and keep the one with the best 3-second hold.
3. Reply to comments with short clips of real friend groups playing (the drawings and Mafia moments are the most shareable).
4. Seed communities where long-distance friend groups gather (study-abroad, diaspora, gaming Discords). Gartic Phone and Jackbox grew through streamers, so send a few small streamers a party code.

## Before scaling marketing
- **The name.** "Houseparty" was a well-known video chat app (bought by Epic Games, shut down in 2021). Run a trademark check before spending on ads.
- **Hosting.** The video says "link in bio", so the app needs to be deployed with a real URL before you post.
